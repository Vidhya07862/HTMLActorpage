Vijay

Vijay, also known as "Thalapathy" Vijay, is a prominent Indian actor and playback singer who primarily works in Tamil cinema. He was born on June 22, 1974, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Vijay made his acting debut as a child artist in the film "Vetri" (1984) and later transitioned to lead roles in the late 1990s.

Over the years, Vijay has established himself as one of the leading actors in Tamil cinema, known for his charismatic screen presence, versatile acting skills, and mass appeal. He has delivered numerous blockbuster films, including "Ghilli" (2004), "Thuppakki" (2012), "Kaththi" (2014), and "Mersal" (2017). Vijay's performances have earned him a massive fan following and several awards, including Tamil Nadu State Film Awards and Filmfare Awards South.
